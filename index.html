Featured project

Criollo

An AI agent for animal rescue foundations. Each intake, vaccination, treatment, transfer and adoption becomes a dated care event, so the full history of an animal can be shown to adopters, donors and partners.

Privacy comes first: animals, shelters and operators are hashed before they are stored, and the design keeps personal data off the chain.

  • Telegram agent built on ElizaOS and the Anthropic Claude API.
  • Care event logger with five event types and 32 categories, backed by PostgreSQL.
  • Verifiable Care Receipt (VCR) design, with a live mint on Solana Devnet and attestations planned on Base.
  • Finalist at the WayLearn x Solana Foundation Hackathon. Now in incubation.
  • Animal Health Passport demo built at the eMerge AI Hackathon 2026 in Miami.
